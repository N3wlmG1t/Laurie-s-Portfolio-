Instructional Video

This video explains a complex topic by starting with familiar concepts and gradually introducing new material.

The audience was resellers who worked with traditional networks but were new to VoIP (Voice Over Internet Protocol). They needed fundamental VoIP knowledge to install our application on customers' networks.

To create this video, I drew on my own experience with network protocols and worked closely with the engineering and support teams to address the most common points of confusion.
