Hi, I'm Laurie.

My work spans troubleshooting references, step-by-step procedures, conceptual overviews, and instructional video, each written for a different audience, from end users clearing a Citrix error to resellers learning VoIP fundamentals for the first time.

Across the samples in this portfolio, I partnered closely with engineering, product, and support teams to find out where people actually get stuck, then wrote documentation that solves the problem in front of them, not just describes the software. Where a diagram or example did the job better than another paragraph, I used one.

A few details in these samples (application names, internal links) have been redacted or renamed to protect confidential information. The writing, structure, and problem-solving underneath are unchanged.
